Welcome to sys0

sys0 lends this machine's spare power to a sys1 pool, for things like websites, media servers, backups and AI.

  • ⚡ Lowest priority, always. Your apps and games come first.
  • 🫥 Invisible. After setup it quietly runs in the background.
  • 🛡️ Sandboxed. Work from the pool runs in locked-down containers.
  • 💸 Free and open source. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Connect to your node

Open the sys1 dashboard on your node, go to Add a machine, and copy the join code.

Whose machine is this?

Encrypted pieces of other machines' backups and app data. You can't read them, and neither can anyone else without the pool's key. sys0 always leaves 5 GB of your disk free.

You're in the pool!

sys0 is now running quietly in the background and starts automatically with this machine. You can close this window.

To leave the pool later, uninstall sys0 or run sys0 uninstall.
