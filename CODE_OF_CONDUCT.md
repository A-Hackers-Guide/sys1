# Code of conduct

**We respect you. Please respect us and everyone else here.**

That means:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Disagree about ideas, never attack people.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks, in any form, for any reason.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Help people find answers instead of making them feel bad for asking.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people, who don't follow this. If something here makes you uncomfortable, open an issue or contact the maintainers privately through GitHub.
